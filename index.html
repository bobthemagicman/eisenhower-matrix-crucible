Eisenhower matrix

Your most important open items, sorted the Eisenhower way and read like a forge. Each quarter of the fire is one quadrant. The hotter a piece glows and the closer it sits to the center, the sooner its next step is due.

      Strike do it now, while the iron is hot. Bank schedule it: bank the fire and come back. Striker delegate it: the striker is the smith’s helper on the sledge. Slag skim it off and drop it.

      Rings mark one week, two weeks, 30 days and 60 days from today. Items with no date sit at black heat on the rim. Selecting a piece highlights its row, and the other way round.
