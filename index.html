Eisenhower matrix

Your most important open items, sorted the Eisenhower way. Each quarter of the scope is one quadrant, and the closer a blip sits to the center, the sooner its next step is due.

    Rings mark one week, two weeks, 30 days and 60 days from today. Items with no date sit on the outer edge. Selecting a blip highlights its row, and the other way round.
